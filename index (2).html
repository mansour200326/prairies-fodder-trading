Who we are

Rooted in the land,
trusted by the herd.

We don't just move bales. We protect the health of every animal that depends on them.

Prairies Fodder Trading is a Dubai-based supplier of premium animal fodder. We bring together carefully sourced hay, grasses and feed from trusted growers across the world's finest forage regions — and deliver them, consistently and on time, to the stables and farms that depend on them.

Every batch is selected for nutrition, freshness and consistency, lab-tested on intake, and traceable from field to gate — because the animals you care for deserve nothing less.

The best feed isn't found. It's selected, tested, and protected — bale by bale.
— The Prairies standard
